Who We Are

OUR
STORY

JMK Autos was founded in 2017 in Ikotun Egbe, Lagos, with one clear conviction: that buying a car in Nigeria should not be a gamble. Too many people were being sold problem vehicles, handed dubious paperwork, and left on their own the moment money exchanged hands.

We started small a handful of carefully selected cars, a small yard, and a big commitment to honesty. Word spread quickly. Customers who came expecting the usual dealer runaround left surprised cars that matched their descriptions, fair prices, and zero document drama.

"We built JMK Autos on one principle: treat every customer the way you'd want your own family treated when buying a car."

JMK Autos Founder

Today, we are one of Lagos' most trusted car dealers with over 500 satisfied customers across Nigeria, a full suite of automotive services, and a team that genuinely cares about getting you into the right vehicle at the right price.
